The channel

LapseOnMaps is a YouTube channel of immersive, day-by-day timelapse maps of historical battles and campaigns: the front lines, the armies and the turning points, with short on-map notes and quiet sound instead of a voice-over.

Watch on YouTube: @LapseOnMaps

ytdesk

ytdesk is the channel's own private desktop tool. The channel owner uses it to upload videos to this channel, edit their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, and read the channel's own YouTube Analytics. It is not a public service and has no other users.
