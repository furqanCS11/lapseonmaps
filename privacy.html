ytdesk Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

ytdesk is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel LapseOnMaps to manage that channel. It is not offered to the public.

What ytdesk accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, ytdesk asks for permission to:

  • upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel (youtube.upload);
  • edit the owner's videos, thumbnails, captions and playlists (youtube.force-ssl);
  • read the owner's YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly).

It also reads public YouTube data (public channel and video statistics) through the YouTube Data API.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to show the owner their own channel's statistics and to upload and edit their own videos.
  • Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. ytdesk has no server and sends data nowhere except to Google's YouTube APIs.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Limited use

ytdesk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. ytdesk uses YouTube API Services and is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting ytdesk's local folder removes everything it stored.

Contact

Questions: contact the channel through youtube.com/@LapseOnMaps.
